## What NOT to Do

❌ **Avoid These Tactics:**
1. Buying links from link farms
2. Participating in link schemes
3. Over-optimized anchor text
4. Low-quality directory spam
5. Comment spam on blogs
6. Automated link building

✅ **Focus On:**
1. Earning editorial links
2. Creating linkable content
3. Building genuine relationships
4. Providing value to partners
5. Natural anchor text variety
6. Quality over quantity

## Monthly Reporting

**Track These Metrics:**
- Total backlinks (increasing)
- Referring domains (unique sites)
- Domain Authority (DA) growth
- DoFollow vs NoFollow ratio
- Link acquisition rate (new/month)
- Lost links (monitor & reclaim)
