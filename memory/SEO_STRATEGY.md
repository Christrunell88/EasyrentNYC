## 📊 Implementation Guide

### 1. Google Ads Campaigns
When setting up Google Ads, add all negative keywords above to:
- Campaign-level negative keyword lists
- Ad group negative keywords where more specific targeting is needed

### 2. Content Strategy
**DO NOT** create content targeting negative keywords. Ensure blog posts, landing pages, and metadata avoid:
- Phrases like "cheap apartments" or "budget rentals"
- References to government housing programs
- For-sale language (buy, purchase, invest)

### 3. Meta Tags & SEO
- Avoid negative keywords in title tags and meta descriptions
- Focus on premium positioning: "luxury," "no broker fee," "quality"

### 4. Social Media
- Maintain premium brand voice
- Avoid engagement with off-topic queries about jobs, roommates, sales
